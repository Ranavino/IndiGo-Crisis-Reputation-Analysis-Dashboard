# IndiGo-Crisis-Reputation-Analysis-Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

The IndiGo Crisis & Reputation Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze brand mentions, engagement, reach, sentiment, media coverage, reputational risk, and customer concerns surrounding IndiGo and its competitors.

The dashboard converts social/media monitoring data into actionable insights for understanding crisis impact, identifying influential sources, tracking negative sentiment, and evaluating reputational risk.

🎯 Business Objectives

Monitor the volume and impact of IndiGo-related mentions.

Identify major crisis events and their potential reach.

Measure engagement, impressions, and engagement rate.

Analyze negative mentions and negative sentiment.

Understand how quickly conversations develop after an incident.

Identify first reporters and influential publications/authors.

Compare media vs. individual sentiment.

Categorize the main concerns and criticisms raised.

Compare reputational risk across platforms.

Analyze IndiGo and competitor mentions.

Identify platforms generating the highest engagement per post.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies
